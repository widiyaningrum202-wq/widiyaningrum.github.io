Root Cause Diagnosis

Borrowers seeking Debt Consolidation under Grade B risk categories possess existing high Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratios. Adding new credit lines while holding active Mortgages ($31.7M defaults) elevates default probabilities.

Underwriting Policy Recommendations

  • Tighten Debt-to-Income (DTI) approval thresholds for Debt Consolidation applicants.
  • Implement automated Early Warning System (EWS) risk alerts for Grade B mortgage holders.
